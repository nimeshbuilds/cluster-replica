# Community code of conduct

Nimesh Builds welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and ways of working. Everyone should be able to ask questions, disagree, and learn without harassment.

## How we work together

- Be respectful and specific. Critique an idea or implementation, not the person.
- Ask for context before assuming intent. Explain technical feedback clearly.
- Give credit, respect privacy, and share only information you have permission to publish.
- Keep discussions relevant. Make room for new contributors and different perspectives.
- Accept reasonable moderation decisions and corrections.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, sexualized content, personal attacks, doxxing, spam, and deliberate disruption are not welcome. Retaliation against someone for raising a concern is also unacceptable.

## Reporting concerns

Use GitHub's **Report content** option on abusive content to make a report to GitHub. Do not post private evidence, personal details, or accusations in a public thread. For a non-sensitive moderation question, contact the maintainer [@npandeya](https://github.com/npandeya) through an appropriate community discussion.

Security vulnerabilities use the separate [security reporting process](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may clarify expectations, edit or remove content, close or lock a discussion, limit participation, or block an account depending on the severity and pattern of behavior. Context and the safety of participants guide the response.

These standards apply to this organization's repositories, issues, pull requests, and discussions, and when someone represents the project in a public community space.
